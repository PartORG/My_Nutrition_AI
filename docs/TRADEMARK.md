# Trademark pre-check: "MyFoodMind" (2026-10-05)

Not legal advice and not a clearance search: a web and app-store sweep to decide whether a full search
(or a rename) is needed before launch. Goods/services: Nice **class 9** (downloadable software) and
**class 44** (nutrition / dietary advice); consider **class 42** (software services) too.

## Found

| Name | What | Risk for MyFoodMind |
|---|---|---|
| **Foodmind: AI Meal Advisor** ([foodmind.org](https://www.foodmind.org/)) | AI nutrition app: photo → calories, allergens, diet recommendations, recipes | **High.** Same dominant word FOODMIND, same goods (AI nutrition app). "My" is a weak prefix that examiners and courts largely ignore. Registration status unknown — check first |
| MindFood AI – Food Tracker (Aerodev SRL, App Store) | AI photo food tracker | Medium: same two words reversed, same goods |
| FoodMinds LLC ([foodminds.com](https://foodminds.com/)) | US food and nutrition communications agency | Low–medium: services (class 35/44-ish), not software, but FOODMINDS ≈ FOODMIND |
| NutriMind AI, Wise Mind Nutrition | Nutrition apps | Low: different dominant word |
| My Food Body Mind | Miami catering | Low: different goods |

No registered "MYFOODMIND" mark turned up in web results; the official registers weren't reachable from
here (interactive search pages).

## Do this before the Play listing goes public

1. **EUIPO eSearch plus** (<https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/>) → Trade marks → search `*foodmind*`,
   `*food mind*`, `*mindfood*`; filter Nice classes 9, 42, 44; status "registered" + "filed".
2. **TMview** (<https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/>) — same queries; covers EU national offices + many others.
3. **USPTO Trademark Search** (<https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/>) — `foodmind`, `food mind`, `mindfood`,
   live marks, classes 9 / 42 / 44.
4. **WIPO Global Brand Database** (<https://branddb.wipo.int/>) — catches Madrid filings (e.g. by Foodmind).
5. Google Play + App Store search: `foodmind`, `food mind`.

Decision rule: a live FOODMIND / FOOD MIND filing in class 9, 42 or 44 in the EU or US → **rename before
launch** (renaming after users and reviews exist costs far more). Nothing live → consider filing an EU mark
for MYFOODMIND in classes 9 and 44 (EUIPO e-filing, ~€850 for the first class + €50 the second, €150 each
from the third).

The app ID `io.github.partorg.myfoodmind` can stay even if the display name changes.
