MyFoodMind

Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

In short: MyFoodMind has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your profiles, meals, chats, health notes and the AI that reads them all stay on your phone. The developer never receives them.

Who is responsible

MyFoodMind is published by Ievgen Perederieiev, an individual developer (the "controller" under the GDPR). Contact: peredereev.ievgen@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, separately for each profile and behind that profile's PIN:

  • Profile name and a salted hash of its PIN (never the PIN itself).
  • Body details you enter for goals: sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level, goal, units.
  • Meals you log, saved recipes, allergies, reminders.
  • Chat messages with the AI assistant, facts it remembers about you (you confirm each one), and an index of past events it uses to remember context.
  • Health information you choose to record (Medium plan): conditions, symptoms, blood glucose, blood pressure and weight readings.
  • Short notes of camera scans ("banana, broccoli"). Photos and camera frames are analysed in memory and never saved or sent.

You can view, edit and delete this data in the app ("What the AI knows about you", the journal, the meal log), delete a profile, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

The AI runs on your phone

The chat assistant (Gemma 4), the search and memory model (EmbeddingGemma) and the food detector (EfficientDet) run on the device. Your messages, photos and health data are not sent to any server, including the developer's. Voice input uses Android's on-device speech recognition; if your phone has no offline speech pack, the app tells you how to add one instead of using online recognition.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you connect it in the Me tab, the app reads your weight (to keep your daily goals current) and your step count (shown on Today) from Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone. It writes nothing there, and what it reads stays on your phone like the rest of your data. You can turn it off in the Me tab or remove the app's access in Health Connect / the Health app at any time.

When the app uses the internet

  • Downloading the AI model. When you tap Download, the chat model (about 2.4 GB) is fetched from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, Hugging Face sees your IP address and standard request details. Nothing about you or your data is sent.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases go through Google Play. The app uses RevenueCat to check which plan is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (random, not linked to your name or email), your purchase history for this app, and basic device and app information. It does not receive any of your profile or health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

When you share something

  • Doctor report. The PDF and CSV are created on your phone and handed to Android's share sheet. You choose where they go (email, Drive, a messenger); the app sends nothing itself.
  • Reporting an AI answer. A long press opens an email draft in your own mail app, containing that answer. Nothing is sent until you send the email.

Backups

Android can back up app data to your Google account. MyFoodMind includes only your profiles and their data in this backup (not the AI models). Google encrypts Android backups; with a screen lock set, they are end-to-end encrypted with your lock-screen credential. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the phone. On the lock screen they show as "contents hidden" and use only a first name, never health details.

Children

MyFoodMind is for adults (18+). A parent may create a profile for a child on their own phone; child profiles use targets the parent sets by hand, never calculated ones.

Legal bases and your rights

Because the developer holds none of your app data, there is nothing to send or delete on a server: the data is under your control on your phone. Health data is processed only on your device, at your explicit request, to provide the features you use. For RevenueCat purchase records you can ask for access or deletion at the contact address above. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date; important changes are also announced in the app.

MyFoodMind gives general nutrition suggestions and records what you log. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
